# 01 — Company

## Purpose

SolenOS exists so **families under progressive caregiving load** keep continuity of responsibility when human attention fragments.

If the entire team disappears, a new organization must understand: we are not shipping a caregiver tip app, dementia chatbot, or task manager. We build **continuity intelligence** — systems that remember who owns what, how load compounds, what decisions were made and why, and what failure is approaching.

## Mission

Reduce caregiver cognitive and operational load by transforming chaos into structured clarity, ownership, and predictive foresight — without medicalizing the caregiver or pretending to be a clinical system.

## North Star (company framing)

- **Product name:** **SolenOS** only (see ADR-015 + `.cursor/rules/solenos-product-identity.mdc`).
- **Category:** Care Reality Intelligence.
- **Foundation:** The Living Care Record.
- **Primary user:** the caregiver (not the patient record, not the clinic).
- **Primary unmet need:** burden reduction and continuity — not more information.
- **Entry market:** dementia / progressive dependency caregiving (market path, not product identity).
- **Long-term vision:** continuity intelligence for family responsibility (`STRATEGIC_ARCHITECTURE` in `architecture-map.ts`).
