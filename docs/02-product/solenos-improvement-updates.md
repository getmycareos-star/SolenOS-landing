# SolenOS Product Directive — Improvement Updates During Active Situations

**Status:** Permanent product behavior  
**Authority:** Product Steward  
**Decision:** **B** — Related improvement outcome event linked to the Active Care Situation (with A’s Clarity update, without premature resolve)  
**Companions:** Situation Relationship · Done-for-now lifecycle · Evidence visibility · Response Behavior

---

## Decision (locked)

> **B — Related event (improvement outcome) linked to the situation.**  
> Include the useful part of **A**: update Care Reality understanding / Clarity without distress framing.  
> Do **not** use **C** (always ask “is it resolved?”).

An improvement is **not** the same as closing or rewriting the situation. Care does not move in straight lines.

---

## Why not A alone

“Update same situation as improving signal” is directionally right but too vague. An improvement is an **event** that needs evidence:

- Event: Ate well today  
- Related to: Reduced eating situation  
- Impact: Positive change observed  
- Source: Caregiver observation  

---

## Why not C

Asking “Is the problem resolved?” on every positive update feels like a form. SolenOS understands first; asks only when necessary.

---

## Behavior

When a caregiver shares an improvement while an Active Care Situation exists (examples are patterns only):

| Existing (illustrative) | New input (illustrative) |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Reduced eating | “She ate well today.” |
| Fall recovery | “She walked to the kitchen today.” |
| Confusion increase | “She seemed more like herself this morning.” |

The system should:

1. Preserve the original situation  
2. Add the improvement as **new evidence / related outcome event**  
3. Update Care Reality State  
4. Adjust understanding from the new information  
5. **Avoid prematurely resolving** the situation  

---

## Never assume

- improvement = resolved  
- one good day = full recovery  
- one positive observation cancels previous concerns  

Lifecycle (Active → Quiet → Resolved → Historical) remains **evidence-based over time**, owned by the Situation Relationship Engine — not by a single positive note or a caregiver close button.

---

## Feel

> "This improvement has been connected to what was happening before."

Not:

> "The old problem disappeared."

---

## Differentiator

SolenOS must remember **both deterioration and recovery**. A notes app stores problems. Care Reality Intelligence understands **change over time**.
